Lesson Summary Guide

1

Asking for Preference

Indefinite Number of Choices
  • • For / About / Regarding [topic], what is your preference?
  • • Regarding [topic], what would you prefer?
  • • Do you have any preference?
Two Choices (A or B)
  • • Concerning [topic], which one would you prefer?
  • • Which suggestion would you prefer?
  • • Which one do you like better? (A) or (B)?
  • • Would you prefer (A) or (B)?
More Than Two Choices
• Which one do you like best?
2

Expressing Preference

  • • I would prefer […]
  • • My preference would be for […]
  • • My preference would be to [verb…]
  • • I would rather choose […] than […]
Rule for Reasons: Always add an explanation using because, as, or since (e.g., "...because it saves time.").
3

Agreeing

  • • I agree with you.
  • • I think you're right.
  • • You've got a point.
Complete the thought: Add explanation with because / as / since to support why you agree!
4

Disagreeing

Direct Disagreement (+ because/as/since)
• Mmm… I'm not sure about that. • I don't think so. • Not me. • I disagree.
Softened / Diplomatic (+ BUT...)

Acknowledge their view first, then use BUT to give your contrasting opinion:

  • • I love that idea, but...
  • • That's true, but...
  • • You may have a point, but...
  • • I see your point, but...
